LibLande Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

LibLande is a personal reference manager and PDF reader for a BibTeX library kept in Google Drive. It is made by Kevin Lande for his own use, and is not offered as a service to others. This policy explains what LibLande does with your information when you sign in with Google.

What LibLande accesses

When you sign in, you allow LibLande to use your Google account with one permission:

  • Google Drive (see, edit, create and delete your files): to read your BibTeX (.bib) library and the PDFs it links to, to build the library LibLande shows, to save the marks you make on PDFs, to keep backup copies, and to save library changes you make.

How it uses it

  • LibLande works only on your own files, and only to do what you ask of it: show your library, open and annotate papers, and save your changes back to your Google Drive.
  • Your library, papers, notes and settings stay in your own Google Drive, and in your browser's storage on your own devices (for example, so LibLande can open offline). LibLande has no server or database of its own.
  • When you look up a DOI, the DOI is sent to Crossref (crossref.org) to fetch the publication's details.
  • LibLande does not collect analytics, does not track you, and shows no advertising.

Sharing

LibLande does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone. Your Google sign-in token is kept only in your own browser, and is used only to reach Google's services on your behalf.

LibLande's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your information

Everything LibLande keeps is in your Google Drive (in your library's folder and a folder named "LibLande") and in your browser's storage. You can delete those at any time. You can withdraw LibLande's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
