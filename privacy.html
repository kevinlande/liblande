LibLande Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

LibLande is a reference manager and PDF reader for a BibTeX library kept in Google Drive. It is made by Kevin Lande, as a personal project, and anyone may use it, free, with their own Google account. This policy explains what LibLande does with your information when you sign in with Google.

What LibLande accesses

When you first sign in, you choose one of two permissions for your Google Drive. LibLande uses either one to read your BibTeX (.bib) library and the PDFs it links to, to build the library LibLande shows, to save the marks you make on PDFs, to keep backup copies, and to save library changes you make.

  • Limited access (see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with LibLande): LibLande can reach only the files and folders you choose for it in Google's own file picker (your .bib, your papers folder and its folders, and your PDFs) and the files it makes itself. The rest of your Google Drive stays out of its reach.
  • Full access (see, edit, create and delete all of your Google Drive files): You choose your .bib and papers folder, and LibLande finds the PDFs in them itself. LibLande still opens only your library's files and its own; Google shows a warning that LibLande hasn't been verified.

You can switch from limited to full access later in Settings › Library. LibLande in Word signs in with the same choice.

LibLande for Google Docs

LibLande for Google Docs is an add-on that cites from your LibLande library in Google Docs. It asks for three permissions:

  • See, edit, create and delete the document it's used in (only that document): to put citations and a References list in it, to find citations you typed or Zotero made, and to keep which works each citation cites, in the document itself.
  • Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars: to show LibLande's panel, which is loaded from LibLande's own site, beside the document.
  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app: to read your LibLande library and its PDFs, to add a publication to your .bib file when you ask, and to make a plain copy of the document when you ask (the first time, you choose the document in Google's file picker).

It uses them only for what you do in it, in the same way as described below; it has no server of its own, and sends nothing about your documents anywhere but Google.

How it uses it

  • LibLande works only on your own files, and only to do what you ask of it: show your library, open and annotate papers, and save your changes back to your Google Drive.
  • Your library, papers, notes and settings stay in your own Google Drive, and in your browser's storage on your own devices (for example, so LibLande can open offline). LibLande has no server or database of its own.
  • Some features look things up in public scholarly services, listed below. Only what the lookup needs is sent: never your files, notes or highlights.
  • LibLande does not collect analytics, does not track you, and shows no advertising.

Services LibLande looks things up in

  • Crossref (crossref.org) and DataCite (datacite.org): a DOI, or a title and author, to fill in a publication's details, and to check for newer versions of forthcoming papers.
  • OpenAlex (openalex.org): a DOI, title or author, to find publications to add, suggest keywords, find free copies of papers (from OpenAlex's open-access data, which includes Unpaywall's), fetch abstracts, and find new papers by authors, in journals, or citing works you choose.
  • Semantic Scholar (semanticscholar.org): a paper's identifier, to recommend related new papers and fetch abstracts.
  • Open Library (openlibrary.org): an ISBN, or a title and author, for a book's details and cover.
  • Citation styles: a style's name, to download it from the Citation Style Language repository on GitHub and the list of styles from zotero.org.
  • Your library's proxy, if you set one up: Find PDF opens an article's address through it, as your library's own site does.

Copy for AI only copies text for you to paste; LibLande sends nothing to an AI service. Reading a scan's text (OCR) happens on your device. LibLande in Word runs inside Microsoft Word, which loads it; its citations are kept in your Word document.

Sharing

LibLande does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone. Your Google sign-in token is kept only in your own browser, and is used only to reach Google's services on your behalf.

LibLande's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your information

Everything LibLande keeps is in your Google Drive (in your library's folder and a folder named "LibLande") and in your browser's storage. You can delete those at any time; Sign out, in LibLande's Settings › Library, removes what it keeps in the browser on that device. You can withdraw LibLande's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
