Set up LibLande

LibLande shows a BibTeX library that’s stored in your Google Drive, in the style of BibDesk. It reads your .bib file and the names of your papers, and never changes them.

ThemeColours and type. Light, Dark or Auto is at the foot of the sidebar.
The paper’s pages in light on dark, on this device (the moon in the reader does the same).
Shared with your other devicesTurned off, it stays on this device only.
Curly quotes in abstractsStraight double quotes (") in abstracts become curly ones (“ ”), so BibDesk stops warning about them.
.bib file
Papers folder

In Google Drive, right-click the file (on iPhone or iPad, tap ⋯) and choose Share, then Copy link.

Leave this empty if your PDFs are in the same folder as the .bib file or in folders inside it, as BibDesk’s AutoFile arranges them.
